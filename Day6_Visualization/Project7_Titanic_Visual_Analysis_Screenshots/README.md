## 📊 Key Visual Insights Discovered

1. **Gender disparity crystal clear in visuals**
   - Women: 74% survival (green bars dominate)
   - Men: 19% survival (red bars minimal)

2. **Wealth = survival visually obvious**
   - Gold (1st class) high, Bronze (3rd class) low
   - Gradient perfectly correlates

3. **Children highlighted in age charts**
   - Under-12 survival spikes visible
   - "Women and children first" proven

4. **Family sweet spot revealed**
   - Peak at 2-4 family members
   - U-shaped curve tells the story

## 🎨 Design Principles Applied

- **Color coding:** Consistent (green=survived, red=died)
- **Annotations:** Highlight key insights
- **Labels:** Clear, bold, readable
- **Grids:** Subtle guides for reading
- **Spacing:** White space prevents clutter
- **Typography:** Bold titles, clear axes
